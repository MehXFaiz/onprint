## What is Foil Stamping?

Foil stamping is a printing technique that applies metallic foil to paper using heat and pressure. The result is a shiny, reflective finish that catches the light and draws attention to specific elements of your design.

### The Process
1. A metal die is created with your design
2. Foil is placed between the die and the paper
3. Heat and pressure are applied
4. The foil adheres to the paper in the desired pattern
5. The excess foil is removed

### Why Choose Foil Stamping?
- Creates premium, luxurious appearance
- Adds visual interest and depth
- Highlights important design elements
- Enhances brand perception
- Makes cards memorable and shareable

## Decision Framework

### Choose Gold Foil If:
- Your brand is traditional and established
- You work in a conservative industry
- Your color scheme is warm
- You want to convey luxury and prestige
- Your target audience values tradition
- You're in real estate, law, or finance

### Choose Silver Foil If:
- Your brand is modern and innovative

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Overusing Foil
Too much foil can look tacky. Use foil strategically to highlight key elements.

### 2. Wrong Color Combination
Ensure your foil choice complements your overall color scheme.

### 3. Ignoring Industry Norms
Research what's common in your industry before choosing.

### 4. Choosing Based on Price Alone
The cheapest option may not be the best for your brand.

### 5. Not Considering Contrast
Ensure foil elements have sufficient contrast with the background.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is gold or silver foil more expensive?
Gold foil is typically slightly more expensive than silver foil, but the difference is minimal.

### Can I use both gold and silver foil on one card?
Yes, combining both foils creates maximum impact but increases cost.

### How long does foil stamping take?
Foil-stamped cards typically take 5-7 business days. Express options are available.

### Will foil stamping work with any design?
Foil stamping works best with bold, simple designs. Fine details may not transfer well.

### Can foil be applied to any paper type?
Foil works best on smooth, coated papers. Some textured papers may not hold foil well.

### Is foil stamping environmentally friendly?
Traditional foil stamping uses metal foils, but eco-friendly foil options are available.
