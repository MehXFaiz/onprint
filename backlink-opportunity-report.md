## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Overview

## 5. Backlink CRM Outreach Workflow

In the ONPRINT Admin SEO Dashboard (`/admin/seo?tab=backlink-opportunities-200`), each opportunity is managed through an **8-stage live pipeline**:

1. **Prospect**: Initial discovery and qualification based on domain authority, location, and topical relevance.
2. **Researching**: Pre-outreach due diligence: identifying contact email, portal submission URL, and exact anchor text fit.
3. **Contacted**: Outreach email or trade license documentation submitted to directory editor.
4. **Submitted**: Profile form filled with verified NAP and official company description.
5. **Approved**: Directory editor or publication accepts the company profile.
6. **Published**: Profile and backlink are live on the target website. Live URL and link attribute (`follow`, `nofollow`) recorded.
7. **Rejected**: Application declined or requires paid sponsorship (discarded to maintain white-hat standards).
8. **Not Relevant**: Target domain changed editorial focus or discontinued external links.
