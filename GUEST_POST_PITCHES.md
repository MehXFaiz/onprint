## Pitch Submission Tips

### Best Practices
1. **Research the Blog**: Read recent articles to understand their style and preferences
2. **Personalize**: Reference specific articles or sections of the blog
3. **Provide Value**: Clearly explain what's in it for their readers
4. **Be Specific**: Include detailed outlines, not just vague ideas
5. **Show Expertise**: Demonstrate knowledge of the Dubai market
6. **Offer Flexibility**: Willing to adapt to their editorial needs

### Follow-Up Strategy
1. **First Follow-Up**: 1 week after pitch
2. **Second Follow-Up**: 2 weeks after first follow-up
3. **Final Follow-Up**: 2 weeks after second follow-up
4. **Response Time**: Allow 1-2 weeks for initial response

### What to Include in Pitches
- Clear article title
- Detailed outline with word counts
- Why it's relevant to their audience
- Your credentials/authority
- What you'll provide (images, data, etc.)
- Timeline for submission
- Sample writing (if available)

### What to Avoid
- Generic pitches sent to multiple blogs
- Vague article ideas without detail
- Overly promotional content
- Poorly written or unprofessional emails
- Ignoring their editorial guidelines

These pitches provide a foundation for your guest posting outreach. Customize each pitch for the specific blog and their audience for best results.
