## Risk Mitigation

**Potential Roadblocks:**

1. **Google Search Console Access Delay**
   - Risk: Cannot connect GSC in week 1
   - Mitigation: Use manual keyword tracking tools (Google Search, SEMrush, Ahrefs) temporarily
   - Backup: Focus on on-page optimization while resolving access

2. **Content Creation Bottleneck**
   - Risk: Cannot write 12 articles in 12 weeks
   - Mitigation: Prioritize top 6-8 articles covering highest-value keywords
   - Backup: Repurpose existing product descriptions into blog content

3. **Backlink Acquisition Slower Than Expected**
   - Risk: Only 5-7 backlinks acquired by day 90
   - Mitigation: Focus on quality over quantity; 5 great links > 15 weak links
   - Backup: Extend outreach to local Dubai events, sponsorships, partnerships

4. **Algorithm Update During Campaign**
   - Risk: Google algorithm change impacts rankings mid-campaign
   - Mitigation: Focus on E-E-A-T principles (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust)
   - Backup: Diversify traffic sources (social, email, paid if needed)

5. **Technical Issues Break Functionality**
   - Risk: SEO changes cause site errors
   - Mitigation: Test all changes on staging environment first
   - Backup: Git version control allows instant rollback
