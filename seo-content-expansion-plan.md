## INTERNAL LINKING STRATEGY

**From Blog to Commercial Pages:**
- Each blog article should link to 6-10 relevant category/service pages
- Use natural, varied anchor text (not always exact-match keywords)
- Link in context where it genuinely helps the reader

**From Commercial Pages to Blog:**
- Add "Related Resources" or "Learn More" sections to category pages
- Link to relevant blog guides from service pages
- Create "Knowledge Hub" section in footer with blog links

**Cross-Linking Between Blogs:**
- Link related blog articles to each other
- Build topic clusters (e.g., all printing technique articles linked together)
