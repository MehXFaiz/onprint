## Cost-Saving Tips

### 1. Order in Bulk
- **Savings**: 40-70% on larger orders
- **Strategy**: Order 6-month supply instead of 1-month
- **Consideration**: Card design stability

### 2. Choose Appropriate Quality
- **Savings**: 30-50% by avoiding over-specification
- **Strategy**: Match quality to use case
- **Consideration**: Don't overpay for features you don't need

### 3. Limit Special Finishes
- **Savings**: 20-40% by reducing finishes
- **Strategy**: Use 1-2 premium finishes instead of 3-4
- **Consideration**: Impact vs. cost balance

### 4. Standard Shapes
- **Savings**: 10-20% by avoiding custom shapes
- **Strategy**: Use standard rectangular cards
- **Consideration**: Custom dies add cost

### 5. Plan Ahead
- **Savings**: 15-25% by avoiding rush fees
- **Strategy**: Order 1-2 weeks in advance
- **Consideration**: Timeline flexibility

### 6. Design Yourself
- **Savings**: AED 50 - AED 200 on design fees
- **Strategy**: Use templates or design yourself
- **Consideration**: Design quality impact

### 7. Compare Printers
- **Savings**: 10-30% by comparing multiple quotes
- **Strategy**: Get quotes from 3-5 printers
- **Consideration**: Quality shouldn't be sacrificed
