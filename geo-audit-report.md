# ONPRINT Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Audit Report

**Company:** ONPRINT (0nprint.com)  
**Location:** Al Quoz, Dubai, UAE  
**Audit Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Audit Scope:** AI Search Visibility for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot

---

## Executive Summary

This audit evaluates ONPRINT's readiness for discovery and citation by AI-powered search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot). The assessment identifies gaps preventing AI systems from clearly understanding the business entity, services, expertise, and value proposition.

**Current GEO Score: 62/100**

### Key Findings

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Strong LocalBusiness schema with accurate NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
- ✅ Comprehensive service and product catalog
- ✅ Homepage FAQs with direct answers
- ✅ Consistent brand entity across pages
- ✅ Technical SEO foundation solid (canonical, hreflang, sitemap)
- ✅ Mobile-responsive design
- ✅ Blog content with structured data

**Critical GEO Gaps:**
- ❌ Homepage lacks clear "What is ONPRINT?" statement for AI extraction
- ❌ Service pages missing direct "What is this service?" definitions
- ❌ No systematic question-answer structure across category/product pages
- ❌ Missing comparison content (digital vs offset, material comparisons)
- ❌ Limited E-E-A-T signals (no author bios, editorial dates minimal)
- ❌ Weak topical clustering and internal semantic linking
- ❌ No AI visibility tracking or monitoring system
- ❌ Product descriptions too short for AI context extraction
- ❌ Missing "Why choose ONPRINT?" section with factual differentiators
- ❌ No structured decision-making content for buyer intent queries
