## 3. Cannibalization Prevention Matrix

To prevent internal competition where multiple URLs dilute authority for the same search queries, ONPRINT enforces strict page routing rules:

| Query Pattern | Designated Canonical Owner | Secondary Reinforcing Pages | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `luxury business cards *` | `/services/business-cards-printing` | Product page `/products/luxury-business-cards` | Service page covers enterprise volume; product page provides self-service e-commerce |
| `custom box printing *` | `/custom-packaging-dubai` | Category hub `/categories/cat-packaging-printing` | Dedicated landing page captures high-intent B2B searchers |
| `commercial printing Dubai` | `/services` | Homepage `/` | Service hub acts as primary commercial portfolio directory |
| `urgent / same day printing *` | `/services` & `/contact` | Specific service pages | Fast response contact points prevent user abandonment |
| `kraft / eco packaging *` | `/eco-friendly-printing-dubai` | Packaging category page | Dedicated sustainability landing page ranks for green commercial tenders |

## 4. Search Intent Distribution

* **Commercial Intent (56%)**: B2B evaluation queries (e.g. `professional printing company Dubai`, `luxury packaging printing Dubai`).
* **Transactional Intent (26%)**: Purchase-ready intent (e.g. `custom box printing UAE`, `luxury business cards Dubai`).
* **Local & Urgent Intent (14%)**: Proximity & timeframe queries (e.g. `same day business cards Dubai`, `urgent printing press Al Quoz`).
* **Informational Intent (4%)**: Educational & specification queries (e.g. `what is the difference between digital and offset printing`).
