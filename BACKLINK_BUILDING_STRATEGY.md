## Link Quality Guidelines

### Do Follow Links
- Prioritize do-follow links (pass link equity)
- Ensure links are relevant to printing/branding
- Check DA (Domain Authority) - aim for 25+
- Verify site is indexed by Google

### Anchor Text
- Use branded anchor text: "ONPRINT", "ONPRINT Dubai"
- Use descriptive anchor text: "printing company in Dubai", "business cards Dubai"
- Avoid exact-match keyword stuffing
- Natural variation in anchor text

### Link Placement
- In-content links (within articles) are most valuable
- Sidebar/footer links have less value
- Avoid link farms or low-quality directories
- Ensure links are from relevant pages

## Risk Management

### Avoid These Practices
- Buying links (against Google guidelines)
- Link farms or low-quality directories
- Excessive reciprocal linking
- Private Blog Networks (PBNs)
- Automated link building tools

### Safe Practices
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Build relationships, not just links
- Create valuable content worth linking to
- Natural link growth
- Diverse link profile
